General
Sports and massage cupping (Hijama) is a therapeutic technique that uses suction cups placed on the skin to lift soft tissue, promote blood flow, and release muscle tension. It is widely used in sports recovery, rehabilitation, and massage therapy to treat pain, stiffness, and soft tissue restrictions.
Your training is delivered by an experienced healthcare professional (20+ years) and holistic therapist (15+ years), trained in Hijama, acupuncture, sports therapy and body alignment, and founder of a top-rated West Midlands clinic, delivering expert clinical education. You are always trained directly by the practitioner, never an assistant or substitute.
The Courses
The 1-2-1 course takes place in person in Birmingham, UK, and offers direct hands-on practice alongside the instructor. The Online Course delivers the same expert-led content live via video call, accessible from anywhere in the world. Both lead to the same certificate.
If you're based in or near Birmingham and value direct hands-on practice, the 1-2-1 Training is ideal. If you prefer flexibility or are based further afield, the Online Course is the better fit. If you're unsure, get in touch and we'll help you decide.
For the Live course, all equipment is provided during the session. For the Online course, we'll send you a recommended equipment list after booking so you can prepare in advance.
