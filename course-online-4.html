The Online Course delivers the full Cupping Academy experience directly to you, wherever you are. Led live by our qualified healthcare professional online, this is not a passive, pre-recorded course — it is a genuine interactive learning experience with real-time guidance and personalised feedback.

Through live demonstration, guided practice, and detailed discussion, you will develop a thorough understanding of cupping (Hijama) theory and technique — all from the comfort of your own space.

Sessions are recorded and shared with you afterwards so you can revisit key content and practise with confidence between sessions.
